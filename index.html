Base Defense / working title

A 3D base-defense game where the player builds and manages magical wards to survive increasingly difficult enemy waves.

My main focus is making the game's systems interact meaningfully: placement geometry, resource generation, defenses, enemy waves, and upgrades all matter in how successful the player is at defending their base.

01Wave logicEnemy spawning and wave progression
02Ward networksPlacement rules and linked stones
03Resource systemsAE generation and spending
04ProgressionUnlocks and upgrade mechanics
Read the technical breakdown +

Wardstones form defensive structures with placement constraints. Their configuration affects how efficiently the player produces the game's resource, AE. Enemy waves put pressure on the player's defenses, while the upgrade system adds progression.

The project is actively evolving. The diagram shown here is a conceptual illustration, not a gameplay screenshot.
